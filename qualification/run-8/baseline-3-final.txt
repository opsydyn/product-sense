**Verdict: Investigate. Don't build yet.** Confidence that a build is premature: moderate to high.

**What we know**
- **Reported:** three organisers say they would use it. That's a compliment, not demand.
- **Unknown:** what goes wrong with email and group messages today. Missed decisions? Hours spent catching up? Volunteers dropping out? We have no observed failures, costs, workarounds or payment commitments.

**Riskiest assumption (hypothesis):** organisers lose enough time or miss enough that they would change habits. The switch looks costly: members stay on email and chat, so the app adds a tool and probably needs access to private group messages. AI summaries are a method, not the value.

**Next step, within 10 days (proposed limit):**
1. Ask the three organisers for recent cases where something was missed, plus the time each one cost.
2. Hand-write a weekly summary from their real threads for two weeks. No code.
3. Track whether they read it, act on it, forward it or ask to keep it.

**Decision rule (proposed):**
- **Build a narrow version** if at least 2 of the 3 rely on it weekly and name a cost it removes.
- **Park it** if they skim it or nothing changes. Revisit if you see real failures.

The engineer should stay on other work until then.
