**Verdict: Investigate. Don't build the app yet.** I'm fairly confident about this.

**What we know**
- **Reported:** three organisers say they would use it.
- **Observed:** they currently coordinate through email threads and group messages.
- **Unknown:** what fails today, how much time it costs, how often it happens, and whether anyone would pay.

Saying they would use it is a weak signal. So far nothing shows that today's approach is failing.

**Hypotheses (not yet checked)**
1. Organisers lose track of decisions, actions or attendance across scattered threads.
2. A weekly summary would cut their admin time or missed follow-ups.
3. They would trust an AI summary and let it read their messages, despite privacy worries.

**Biggest risk:** the problem may be too small, or too rare, to make people switch from email.

**Next step:** spend 1–2 days, not ten, on learning:
- Ask each organiser to walk through a recent week. Look for missed items, how long things took, and any workarounds.
- Then write summaries by hand for 2–3 weeks.
- Track whether they read them and act on them, and whether they would pay or ask for more.

**Keep going if** summaries are used every week and replace real work. **Stop if** usage fades or the summaries add nothing new. Only spend the engineer's time once that evidence exists.
