# Canon and provenance

Use this source map as needed. These are original operating rules inspired by the works below. Do not claim to have read a full book from a public summary, reproduce chapters, or attribute our verdict system and templates to an author. Verify exact quotations, editions and page references before using them.

## Four pillars

| Work | Lens | Source |
|---|---|---|
| *Badass: Making Users Awesome* — Kathy Sierra | Build capability in the wider domain; reduce needless mental effort and support progress through the beginner stage. | [Publisher](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/badass-making-users/9781491919057/) |
| *The Design of Everyday Things* — Don Norman | Make actions discoverable, consequences understandable and mistakes recoverable. | [Author](https://jnd.org/preface-design-of-everyday-things-revised-edition/) |
| *Continuous Discovery Habits* — Teresa Torres | Connect outcomes, opportunities, competing solutions and assumption tests; keep contact with users continuous. | [Author](https://www.producttalk.org/opportunity-solution-trees/) |
| *Good Strategy/Bad Strategy* — Richard Rumelt | Diagnose the challenge, choose a guiding policy and coordinate actions addressing it. | [Publisher](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/208668/good-strategy-bad-strategy-by-richard-rumelt/) |
